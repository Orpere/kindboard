# ADR-0025 — Light theme logos: original brand colors, recolored light lettering

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Supersedes:** ADR-0024 (its light-theme treatment is replaced; dark themes
  are unchanged)

## Context

ADR-0024 made the Light theme render every tool logo and the brand mark with a
dark multiplicative tint (`0x2f3a45`) so white lettering stayed visible on the
pale background (`bg 0xf5f7fa`).

User report (follow-up): **the Light theme icons are all black — the logos
lost their brand colors and turned into dark monochrome silhouettes; the
lettering inside them cannot be told apart.** Requirement: logos must read as
their normal colorful selves on Light, while white/very-light lettering must
still be visible — i.e. the fix must not reintroduce white-on-light (the
ADR-0024 bug) and must not keep the all-black look.

## Root cause

A single multiplicative tint cannot satisfy both constraints at once:

- identity (`WHITE`) → original colors, but kind/kindboard-brand/ cilium
  lettering (near-white artwork) vanishes on pale surfaces;
- dark tint → lettering visible, but **every** logo becomes a dark silhouette.

Pixel analysis of the embedded artwork (visible pixels, WCAG contrast vs Light
`bg`, ITU-R BT.709):

| logo | fails on Light with identity tint | nature of the artwork |
|---|---|---|
| docker | 23% (minor interior details) | blue whale, fine |
| helm | 0% | dark navy, fine |
| k9s | 9% | black letters, fine |
| kubectl (kubernetes) | 42% — white wheel *inside* the blue heptagon, fine | standard logo, reads on white everywhere |
| kustomize | 19% — white text *inside* the dark-blue box, fine | standard logo |
| **kind** | **69%** | light-blue letters (lum ≈ 0.72) on transparent |
| **brand mark** | **69%** | same artwork family as kind |
| **cilium** | **59%** | white hexagon lattice (lum ≈ 1.0) on transparent |

Only three marks need treatment: kind, the brand mark, and cilium.

## Decision

1. **Every theme paints logos with the identity tint** (`logo_tint = WHITE`
   in all three palettes): the original brand colors return on Light, and
   Dark/HighContrast keep their historical look (the artwork was always
   painted with the identity tint there — nothing changes).
2. **Light-theme load-time recolor for the three white-lettering marks**
   (`icons::LightRecolor`): while uploading the texture, near-opaque pixels
   (alpha > 200) brighter than a per-mark luminance threshold are replaced by
   the Light theme's dark neutral `0x2f3a45` (alpha preserved; translucent
   anti-aliased edges keep their color so shapes keep blending):

   | mark | `max_light_lum` | recolored | preserved |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | kind | `0.45` | light-blue letters (lum ≈ 0.72) | navy details, orange accent (lum ≈ 0.20) |
   | brand mark | `0.45` | same as kind | same |
   | cilium | `0.80` | white lattice (lum ≈ 1.0) | colored accents (lum ≤ 0.65) |

3. **Textures are cached per theme** (`tool-logo-{id}-{theme}` /
   `brand-mark-{theme}`), so switching themes at runtime reloads the correct
   pixels without an app restart.
4. Recolor thresholds/target live as constants in `icons.rs` and are locked by
   unit tests (threshold behavior on real logo colors, target ≥ 3:1 on Light
   `bg`).

## Consequences

- Light theme: colorful, recognizable logos; dark lettering where the artwork
  is light. Dark themes: unchanged.
- `Palette.logo_tint` is `WHITE` in all three palettes (identity); its doc
  points at the load-time recolor for Light-theme readability.
- Regression tests: `logo_tint_contract_per_theme`,
  `light_logo_recolor_target_contrasts_with_light_bg`, and the `icons::tests`
  recolor suite (near-opaque-only, alpha-preserving, per-mark thresholds,
  only-kind-and-cilium).
- ADR-0024's `logo_tint_values_are_stable` /
  `logo_tint_contrasts_with_bg_in_every_theme` are replaced by the tests above.
